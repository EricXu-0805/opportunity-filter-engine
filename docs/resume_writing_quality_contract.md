# Résumé writing quality: local evidence contract (w14.1)

Scope: `/api/tailor`, `/api/tailor/renovate`, and `/api/tailor/bullet`. This supplements the historical `tailor_boundary_report.md`; that report describes an earlier implementation, not the current storage/export system.

## w14.1: evidence-mapped rewrites, each in its own language

`backend/lib/evidence_map.py` holds the contract these routes and full-target v6 share.

- The server cuts numbered anchors from the target: the description (a faculty directory template only through its source-stated `metadata.research_areas_raw`), requirements unless inferred, and recent-works titles that pass the publication trust gate. URLs, e-mail addresses and posting boilerplate are cut out. A target with no quotable text gets no model call; every bullet comes back kept with `target_has_no_text`.
- One generation call per request, ending at most 40 s into it. The model links phrases of each bullet to anchor words and declares its moves: `lead_with`, `relabel` (a "same" link only), `verb_first`, `personal_first` or `tighten`. Broader and cross-language relabels and trimming are not offered (calibration, 2026-09-30).
- Each bullet is rewritten only in its own language: an English bullet in English and a Chinese one in Chinese, whatever the UI locale, which picks only the language of the model's instructions and of the server's explanations. There is no translation move (owner decision, 2026-10-02). A rewrite in another language than its original, or of a line whose current wording is in another language than its original, is kept as written (`beyond_allowed_edit`) before the claim locks and the review. The script decides too: a rewrite that drops all of its line's Chinese ("负责 data cleaning 和 deployment" -> "Cleaned data and deployment") or adds Chinese to a line without any is in another language, whatever the word count says. So is a rewrite that adds a letter of any other script the line does not already use (katakana, Hangul, Cyrillic, full-width Latin) or drops one the line uses ("Python 데이터 파이프라인" -> "Python data pipeline": language() and the tokens read neither, so every such letter keeps its count), a relabel whose "from" and "to" are not in the same script ("脑电 signal" -> "brain signal", "데이터 파이프라인" -> "data pipeline"), and a rewrite of a line written in part in a non-Latin script (CJK, Hangul, kana, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Thai, Devanagari; an accented Latin letter is no such script) that gains an English function word, or of an English line with Chinese that gains a Chinese function character, beyond what a relabel's "to" holds ("负责 A 和 B" -> "负责 B and A", "Python 및 SQL" -> "SQL and Python"). A rewrite of a line `language()` reads as English is shown only when the line shows itself English: two different English function words of three letters or more outside the phrases the rewrite renames, read on the line's ASCII words whatever name or sign of another script it also holds, and neither "via" nor a word joined to another by a hyphen (`_english_line`, rounds 4 and 5). Without them the line is kept as written (`english_unproven`).
- A rewrite may add only words of its own evidence plus a declared relabel's term; it passes the relabel-term filters, the claim locks (actor, qualifier, intent, status, setting, denial, team, publication) and one fail-closed faithfulness review that also judges each used link. Only an accepted rewrite is shown; every other bullet comes back as written with its reason (`no_link`, `already_aligned`, `no_safe_change`, `cosmetic_only`, `beyond_allowed_edit`, `rewrite_rejected`, `review_rejected`, `review_unavailable`, `model_unavailable`).
- A relabel renames only what its link's source names: its "from" lies inside that source, and the swap may not lose or add a number, a word of any lock family or a personal marker. The lock families cover team and shared credit (including a co- word such as "co-authored", 合著, 合写 or 联合 before a verb, and collaborators named by "other", "another" or "fellow": "with three other students", "alongside two other volunteers", 另外三名同学; co-located, 联合利华, 北京联合大学, 联合培养, 联合实验室 and 联合航空 are no credit), a share of the work ("participated in", "contributed to", "involved in", "took part in", 参与, 参加, 贡献), help, negation and denial (with unpublished, unsubmitted, unfinished, untested, unverified, unvalidated and unreviewed), publication, intent, unfinished work (including "in development", a Chinese verb + 中 such as 开发中, 即将 and 将在), a thing's status or nature ("planned", "draft", "prototype", "simulated", 初稿, 原型), approximation and span (the English span words in any use: "about", "under", "less than", "fewer than", "as many as", "upwards of", "close to"; the hedges approximately, roughly, nearly, almost, at least, at most, since, until and per, "or so", "-odd", ~, ≈, <, >, ≤, ≥ and a trailing +; and 约, 将近, 超过, 至少, 左右, 至多, 多达, 高达, 最多 where it does not close a clause, 不到, 余 after a number, 余 or 多 after 十, 百, 千 or 万, 上百, 上千, 上万, 起, 以来 and 至今), solo work, limits and another person's part (a revision word in any use). No move adds "I", "my", 本人 or 我 ("My" at the start of a sentence counts). A number keeps the sign that bounds or approximates it ("~300", ">90%", "40+"), and a 将 that says "will" (将于 5 月发表) is a word, so no move adds or drops either. Each publication status, shared credit ("with two teammates", 与组员一起) and since/until stays on its own work or action. The claim locks read the rewrite as written. The version without the posting's terms undoes each relabel where its "to" stands, once and as whole words ("EEG data", not the start of "EEG database"); two relabels to one term, or a "to" that stands twice, give no such version, since undoing them could swap the student's words. That version is a line the student may be shown, so it goes to the same review as a pair of its own and is offered only on its own accepted verdict.
- The actor lock reads a bare role noun that opens a line as the student's own title ("TA for CS 124, holding office hours", "Research technician in the Smith Lab", 研究生期间负责): it names no other doer. With a determiner, as an abbreviated title, or with its own verb right after it, a role noun is someone else ("The nurse, with my help, recorded", "the postdoc in our lab ran", "Dr. Lee designed", "Lab technician ran the assays", 导师负责). A verb after a dash or a bracket follows a heading ("TA — holds office hours"), and a past form right after the role noun followed by "by" or a preposition describes the title ("TA supervised by Prof. Lee", "Technician trained in PCR"); after a name it is that person's action ("Professor Lee presented at the conference"). A pronoun ("They analyze", "He then wrote") or a plural role noun is never a title, and a role noun followed by nothing but a name is a person whose verb stands in a later clause ("Professor Lee, who leads the lab, designed"). Not read as another doer, and left to the review: a Chinese role noun with an adverb or a second person before its verb (导师亲自设计, 导师和博士生设计), and a bare role noun with a past form and a preposition ("PI applied for the grant", "Supervisor worked on the assay"), which no rule tells from "Technician trained in PCR" without a new list. "The scheduled maintenance" is routine work; "a planned study" and "a study scheduled for May" are still planned. Only "scheduled for" or "scheduled to" reads as planned now, so "a scheduled clinical trial of 40 patients" -> "a clinical trial of 40 patients" is left to the review (`scripts/scheduled_probe.py`; a narrower alternative would add an entry to a frozen list).
- A relabel also keeps every lock word over the whole line, not only inside each swap, so a lock word split across two relabels (初步 in 生的初 -> 生的实 and 步结果 -> 验结果) or a limit word left outside any ("just") keeps the line as written. Added Chinese is read both character by character and as the words its adjacent characters form (公司 in 已部署到公司生产系统 is a setting). A number keeps a content word beside it, as the line has it, with the relabels undone, so two numbers cannot swap actions through a lead_with ("by 45% ... by 12%" -> "by 12% ... by 45%"); a number that opens its clause was fronted with its own phrase and is left alone. A team heading that names an action ("与两名同学合作搭建了气象站，本人单独编写了…") is shared work: dropping the 本人 after it needs personal_first, under any declared move.
- `personal_first` keeps the student's own part a clause of its own: joined to the shared part's credit ("Wrote the backend in Flask and built the website with a friend") it is kept as written; a team named as its own doer ("for a charger that our team designed") is not shared credit.
- In full target, a confirmed support line binds its qualifiers only to the words a rewrite takes from it: "与导师一起组织了 40 场访谈" asks nothing of a reordered "Built the website with a friend; I wrote the backend in Flask", which takes no word from it. A rewrite that takes words from a support line is read with it, as before.
- `status_upgraded` also reads an -ing word the verb list does not know that leads a clause as under way when the rewrite writes its -ed form ("Wiring 3 soil sensors" -> "wired"), reads set, put, cut and read in a rewrite as past when the original has them only in their -ing form ("Setting up a server" -> "set up"), and counts the Chinese status words of each kind, so a reorder may not drop 将于 beside 目前正在. An -ing word the list does not know that names a role ("Visiting student", "Fundraising chair") leads no work under way.
- `source_bullets` holds at most 6,000 characters in all, the size of 12 bullets of 500. The contract and the claim locks run on a worker with a 10 s deadline, past which the originals are kept unchecked, and the claim locks do not read main's attribution parser. `tests/test_rewrite_cpu_bounds.py` holds each unit of 15 adversarial shapes at 6,000 characters, in Tailor and full-target form, alone and with a support line, to 0.25 s of process time for the contract and the locks together, and requests at the cap through `/api/tailor`, `/api/tailor/bullet`, full target and the selection plan to a 0.25 s gap between `/api/tailor/status` answers. Before any worker, a request is bounded too: `original_bullets` and `source_bullets` hold at most 200 items, checked before their items are validated, and a 422 names at most 20 errors; a body is refused before it is parsed when it holds more lists and objects, or more commas between items, than its route's bounds, both counted outside JSON strings (`backend/lib/request_body.py`: 10,000 lists and objects for `/api/tailor`, `/bullet` and `/renovate`, 100 for the two extraction routes and 20,000 for the two full-target routes; 50,000 commas, and 60,100 on the extraction routes); a full-target draft is validated and prepared on the request lane, and the selection plan's answer is parsed there (`scripts/request_body_containers.py`, `scripts/worst_inputs_lag.py`, `scripts/request_parse_lag.py`, `scripts/plan_output_lag.py`). A chain of "and"/"or" gerunds is followed once, not once from each gerund in it. No pattern can read a run in two ways, a run of spaces or of 为 starts one scan, and a line's personal markers and settings are read once (tested to 60,000 characters).
- The selection plan (`full-target-plan-v5`) proposes no wording. Every line a student is shown passes the review, and the plan makes one call with none behind it, so it advises keep, compress or omit with its reasons, its prompt asks for `rewrites: []`, and a rewrite the model returns anyway is checked for shape and dropped. Its version moved from v4 because main's v4 plan carries unreviewed shorter wording: the browser refuses any other version and any item that carries a rewrite, and it shows and applies no plan wording at all.
- The browser shows a rewrite only from a reviewed response. `/tailor/status` must report `w14` or later; a Tailor bullet is shown as rewritten only with `status: "rewritten"`, and a re-optimized or renovated variant only from a response that says so, so a browser that meets an older backend during a deploy shows the student's own lines. A restored saved renovation opens each bullet at its own text unless its current variant is the student's own edit or carries the w14 stamp (`reviewed`), its ops (never `translate`) and the bullet's script (Chinese in both or neither, the server's rule); older variants stay in the stored history, where no rollback or roll-forward step, copy-all, history preview or save-conflict preview reaches them (each reads the bullet's own text instead). New variants carry the stamp of the rules that reviewed them. A Tailor draft saved from rewrites before w14 (origin `reviewed_output`, rules below w14) says so before the student confirms its lines and offers to start again from the résumé; a full-target line whose last AI record has no full-target-v6 check says it was not fact-checked, beside "Restore original".
- `/tailor/extract-bullets` and `/tailor/structure`, their no-model fallback and the section headings they return are origin/main's (round 4): a model line is accepted when it is contiguous résumé text, and the section heading is the model's, as on main. A cut can part a status from its claim ("Planned to survey 50 farmers ..." -> "survey 50 farmers ...", or a bullet without its "(Under Review)" row); that risk is main's too, and the model review of each rewrite is the guard for it. `scripts/extraction_differential.py` compares both routes with origin/main's on the same résumés and model replies, through the route functions and through each module's own router.
- The student's profile, confirmed skills and interests stay in the prompt as direction, never evidence. After "Use kept as new originals", `source_bullets` carries each line's evidence so reviewed wording never becomes evidence; the saved draft keeps those sources across a reload.

## Why each line stays in its own language

w14.0 let a rewrite translate its line into the UI language, and it checked translations with the same closed word lists that guard same-language rewrites. Its review rounds did not converge: blockers and majors per review ran 4, 1, 1, 3, 4 and 8 (`recount.py`). A root-cause analysis of the branch as it stood on 2026-10-02 (its scripts are outside the repository) found translation to be the main cause:

- Over 2,182 corpus and reviewer-probe pairs, translations were 179 of the 180 faithful lines the locks refused as fabrication, and 211 of the 255 traps only the review stopped (`recount.py`).
- All 29 behavioural findings of the last four reviews involved a translation, including all 12 blockers and majors (`recount.py`).
- 18 of the 21 patterns the translation families read were the same objects that the relabel checks or the same-language claim locks read, so each translation fix moved other results (`shared_families.py`). Across the rounds the translation contract grew from 28 to 162 logic lines, while the same-language contract went from 196 to 220 (`subsystems.py`).

The same-language checks held: their claim locks stopped 22 of the 35 same-language corpus traps that pass the contract and refused no faithful corpus line (`recount.py`, on a4849a1; the round-1 counts are below); of the reviewers' probes they refused one faithful line, the lab-technician line, which the role-noun reading above now passes. So a rewrite stays in its line's language. If students turn out to need translation, it comes back as a separate, labelled step with its own calibrated review, never inside a rewrite.

CPU was a separate cause: the claim locks also ran main's attribution parser (`cpu_probe.py`). Deleting that reading changed none of the 690 corpus outcomes (`parser_ablation.py`), so the locks no longer read it.

## Round 3 (2026-10-03): the gaps the re-measure reported, and the counts after the fixes

1. No line is shown without the review.
   - Verdicts: `ai_review` counts an answer only when its verdict list has one entry per pair and the n-th entry is an object numbered n; anything else rejects every pair of the batch.    - Wrapped bullets: `_resume_rows` decides once per row whether it opens an item.    - It goes on with the bullet when the row above cannot end an item (it ends on a determiner or a leading word: the claim locks' `_NOT_HEAD` and `_OBJECT_END`; or on 并/和/及), when it opens with a joining word (`evidence_map._FUNCTION_EN`), or when it carries a status, a share of the work or a negation, whatever its shape ("Under Review", "Status: under review", "In progress, Jan 2025 - Present", "尚未投稿").    - Otherwise a row starts one only when it is shaped as one: a heading (`_own_row`, or 2-8 CJK letters alone: "实习经历"), a title and its details ("Campus Food Pantry Dashboard, Spring 2024"), a label ("Technical Skills: Python, R, SQL"), a role or date row (`experience_evidence.names_no_action`), or a CJK row ending in its dates ("研究助理 2025年1月至今"). ## Frozen lists

- The lock lists are frozen: every pattern and word table that the claim locks (`target_resume_ai_grounding.py`) and the contract's checks (`evidence_map.py`) read. `tests/test_lock_lists_frozen.py` pins each family's entry count, so adding or dropping an alternative, a listed character or a whole family fails it, and a digest of each family's content (a pattern's text and flags, a table's sorted items), so a word swapped for another, which keeps the count, fails it too. Logic that reads a family is not pinned, so a lock's diff still needs reading. The actor lock holds no inline regex, but it does hold an inline word tuple (he, she, they, which the frozen `_OTHER_SUBJECT` already matches) and a character set (`_'’.`) that narrow the role-noun title reading; neither reads anything as a lock beyond 0cbb78c. `_BROKEN_RULE_TAG` (in NOT_LOCKS: it parses the review's verdict) gained 【 and 规则 without a calibration run; it can only add review rejections.
- A same-language shape a reviewer finds goes to the review's calibration set and to the PR's limits, not into a list.
- A lock changes only to remove a measured faithful refusal or a CPU path. The commit carries the measurement and updates the pinned count and digest.
- The review prompt changes only together with a new run of the 170-pair calibration (85 traps and 85 faithful pairs, 3 samples each), and the commit carries its result. The same test pins the prompt's digest; that prompt calibrated at 0/255 trap accepts and 17/255 faithful rejects.

## What changed

The old vocabulary check pooled a student's profile and all submitted bullets. It could accept project A's technology or number in project B, and accept “I led” when the original said “I did not lead.” Controlled provider-free HTTP tests reproduced both errors in the real routes.

Each rewrite now uses the corresponding original bullet as its evidence:

- `/tailor`: the source selected by the existing `source_index` association.
- `/tailor/renovate`: that foregrounded bullet's `base_text`; another bullet or a profile skill is not evidence of work on this project.
- `/tailor/bullet`: `base_text` when provided. The editable `current_text` is sent separately to guide phrasing, not to establish additional facts. Older callers that omit the base provide their current bullet as the only source; this is caller-supplied evidence, not independent verification.

`source_evidence` is checked against the same local original. An unrelated or fabricated quote is removed instead of displayed as proof. The known concrete-term/number validator and the same conservative EN/ZH claim locks used by full-target AI apply to these three routes. Missing detail is not filled from the student's skill list, another project or the opportunity's vocabulary.

On rejection, `/tailor` keeps its existing original fallback/partial-suggestion behavior; Renovate leaves the original bullet and its rollback floor intact; single-bullet optimization returns the user's `current_text` unchanged with `changed=false` and a warning. A rejected suggestion does not replace manual edits with the base text. Saving and confirmation flows are unchanged.

## Target relevance and missing information

Single-bullet optimization now receives the detached public target's professor name, organization and description excerpt. The description uses the existing 1,200-character budget and whitespace sanitization; When the clean field is absent, the already-public `description_raw` is allowed as a fallback; no unprojected collector record or contact-reveal payload is read here. The prompt separates target relevance from student evidence and treats these fields as data, not instructions. Existing output/token/time/provider-call budgets are unchanged.

The model should emphasize a supported action, method or result relevant to the public research description. A general skill does not establish its use on this project. When a source lacks personal role, method or a supported outcome, preserve the stated contribution; students can supply and confirm facts through the existing full-résumé supplement flow. This change does not add an automatic question generator or a professor-paper retrieval system.

## Evidence and limits

`tests/test_resume_writing_quality.py` supplies controlled model outputs through the real HTTP handlers. It checks cross-project technology/number leakage; profile-only skill transfer; English and Chinese negation/team/publication upgrades; truthful reordering; local quotations; retained manual wording; and bounded public research context. Existing parser/index/localization positive fixtures now contain the actual actions their generated examples claim. Their positive assertions remain; the old vague source-to-stronger-action pairs are retained as negative cases.

These are bounded regression checks, not semantic entailment. Exact sensitive-clause preservation can reject a valid paraphrase or translation; unlisted action synonyms, generic claims and a mismatched relationship between already-present numbers can still escape token checks. Keeping the original available is safer than silently asserting a stronger contribution. The full-target pipeline already has independent confirmed-entry evidence and uses the same claim locks. The shared helper now separates explicit English/Chinese contrast clauses, so a negative first clause cannot exempt a new affirmative responsibility/publication claim after “but” or “但”. This fix also applies to full-target suggestions and is covered by their existing regressions plus the new paired receipt cases.

No real model output or human résumé review was evaluated here. Naturalness, useful professor/lab alignment, bilingual rewrite quality and follow-up question quality still require fixed real samples with human judgments. Passing these tests does not complete M36/M38–M40 or prove that generated résumés are ready to send.
