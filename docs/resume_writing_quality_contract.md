# Résumé writing quality: local evidence contract (w14.0)

Scope: `/api/tailor`, `/api/tailor/renovate`, and `/api/tailor/bullet`. This supplements the historical `tailor_boundary_report.md`; that report describes an earlier implementation, not the current storage/export system.

## w14.0: evidence-mapped rewrites

`backend/lib/evidence_map.py` holds the contract these routes and full-target v6 share.

- The server cuts numbered anchors from the target: the description (a faculty directory template only through its source-stated `metadata.research_areas_raw`), requirements unless inferred, and recent-works titles that pass the publication trust gate. URLs, e-mail addresses and posting boilerplate are cut out. A target with no quotable text gets no model call; every bullet comes back kept with `target_has_no_text`.
- One generation call per request, ending at most 40 s into it. The model links phrases of each bullet to anchor words and declares its moves: `lead_with`, `relabel` (a "same" link only), `verb_first`, `personal_first`, `tighten`, or `translate` when the bullet is not in the UI locale's language. Broader and cross-language relabels and trimming are not offered (calibration, 2026-09-30).
- A rewrite may add only words of its own evidence plus a declared relabel's term; it passes the relabel-term filters, the claim locks (actor, qualifier, intent, status, setting, denial, team, publication) and one fail-closed faithfulness review that also judges each used link. Only an accepted rewrite is shown; every other bullet comes back as written with its reason (`no_link`, `already_aligned`, `no_safe_change`, `cosmetic_only`, `beyond_allowed_edit`, `rewrite_rejected`, `review_rejected`, `review_unavailable`, `model_unavailable`).
- A relabel renames only what its link's source names: its "from" lies inside that source, and the swap may not lose or add a number, a word of any lock family or a personal marker. The lock families cover team and shared credit (including a co- word such as "co-authored", 合著, 合写 or 联合 before a verb, and collaborators named by "other", "another" or "fellow": "with three other students", "alongside two other volunteers", 另外三名同学; co-located, 联合利华, 北京联合大学, 联合培养, 联合实验室 and 联合航空 are no credit), a share of the work ("participated in", "contributed to", "involved in", "took part in", 参与, 参加, 贡献), help, negation and denial (with unpublished, unsubmitted, unfinished, untested, unverified, unvalidated and unreviewed), publication, intent, unfinished work (including "in development", a Chinese verb + 中 such as 开发中, 即将 and 将在), a thing's status or nature ("planned", "draft", "prototype", "simulated", 初稿, 原型), approximation and span (the English span words in any use: "about", "under", "less than", "fewer than", "as many as", "upwards of", "close to"), solo work, limits and another person's part (a revision word in any use). No move adds "I", "my", 本人 or 我 ("My" at the start of a sentence counts). A number keeps the sign that bounds or approximates it ("~300", ">90%", "40+"), and a 将 that says "will" (将于 5 月发表) is a word, so no move adds or drops either. Each publication status, shared credit ("with two teammates", 与组员一起) and since/until stays on its own work or action. The claim locks read the rewrite as written; a relabel is undone, for the version without the posting's terms, where its words stand whole ("EEG data", not the start of "EEG database").
- A translation is checked in both directions for numbers (month names count), names, each qualifier family (negation includes untested, unverified, unvalidated and unreviewed; the team family reads "team", "with two teammates", "jointly", co- credit, 与组员一起 and 一起, but not collaborators named by "other", "another" or "fellow", which only a relabel locks) and the work's status, and for any setting, quality or relevance claim the other line lacks; the claim locks compare those three only within one language.
  - Status is four families that must appear on both sides or neither: planned or hoped for ("plan to", "a planned study", "scheduled for", 计划, 预定于), under way or still to come ("in progress", "under development", "will", "to appear", 正在, 开发中, 即将, 将于), still to come on its own ("will", "upcoming", "forthcoming", "to appear", "in press", "expected", "anticipated", 预计, 即将, 将于, 将在, 将会, 将要; not 将在线, 将会议, 将会员, 将要点, 将要求 or 将要素, where 将 marks the object), so 即将发表 beside 目前 keeps its own word, and a draft (a draft as a thing: "a draft manuscript", "(draft)", 初稿, 草稿; not the verb in "Draft weekly newsletters").
  - An English line led by the -ing form of a verb from the résumé verb list ("Developing a dashboard"; not "Using" or "Applying", which name a method, and not an -ing word the list does not know, such as "Fine-tuning", "Visiting" or "Fundraising") pairs with 正在 or 目前 on the Chinese leading verb (after a subject or a time word: 本人, 我们, 本学期, 今年暑假, 目前我, 现) or a verb + 中 that ends the first clause, bracketed or not (预约系统（开发中）), when the Chinese has no other under-way word. The clause that holds the Chinese leading verb carries no done mark; it is the first clause that is more than a lead marker, so 目前，开发了网站 and 项目进行中，开发了网站 hold it in their second clause. A done mark elsewhere needs that marker, and no more parts of the Chinese (split at clause punctuation, 、 and 并) may carry one than the English has clauses that open with a finished verb ("...; wrote a review") or a headline's noun and a known past verb that names no status ("Paper accepted at CHI", "preprint posted", not "release expected" or "homepage planned").
  - The done marks are 了, 已 and 完成 (not the 了 of 为了, 除了 or 了解, the 已 of 已在, or the 完成 of 正在完成 or 未完成). Other ways Chinese says a work is done (开发过, 曾, 开发出, 建成, 上线, 投入使用, 定稿, 结束) are no done mark: read as one, they also matched 造成, 线上线下 and 持续交付, so the review judges them.
  - English work under way or planned takes a done mark only in as many Chinese parts as it has such finished clauses ("under development" is never 目前已上线). A Chinese line with 正在, or a verb + 中 that does not describe a thing (开发中的), and no done mark may not become English with a clause that opens with a finished verb or with one noun and a past verb ("...; tested it", "Paper accepted at CHI"). A finished verb after a subject or two nouns ("it launched in March", "the homepage went live", "Marketing campaign ... launched") and a finished state ("is now complete", "already online") are not read; the review judges them. A verb + 中 within four characters after 在, 到 or 入 is a place (在项目开发中).
  - Another person's part must carry over, including a revision or review: "; Sam revised it", "their revised version", "; Sam, a senior student, revised it", "reviewed by the lab manager", "a senior student corrected it", 学长, 主管. The student's own revision need not: "Revised the manual", "Carefully revised it", "We revised it", "and revised it", "I edited"; "the revised proposal" and "my revised plan" name a version.
  - "About", "around", "over", "under", "more than", "less than", "up to", "upwards of", "close to", "as many as", "as much as", "as high as" and "some" qualify only before a quantity ("about 40", "about twice", "under several dozen", "some 30"); the hedges (approximately, roughly, nearly, almost, at least, at most, since, until, per), "or so", "-odd", ~, ≈, <, >, ≤, ≥ and a trailing + always do. The Chinese spans are 约, 将近, 超过, 至少, 左右, 至多, 多达, 高达, 最多 (not where it closes a clause, 得票最多), 不到 wherever it stands (用不到 100 行, and the denial in 找不到), 余 after a number, 余 or 多 after 十, 百, 千 or 万, 上百, 上千, 上万, 起, 以来 and 至今. "Fewer than", "as few/little/low as", "estimated", 近, 以上, 以下 and 多 after a digit are no span in a translation (they also read "Estimated 3 models", 靠近, 2% 以下 for "below 2%" and GPT-4 多模态). A count word, a compound or a preposition is not a qualifier there: 12 只小鼠, 起草, 发起, 预约, 参考文献, 拟南芥, "a survey about sleep".
  - A share of the work carries over: "participated", "contributed", "involved in", "involvement" or "took part" needs 参与, 参加 or 贡献, and 参与 or 贡献 needs one of those English words; 参与者, "participants" and 有 50 名被试参与的实验 name other people.
- In one language, status_upgraded also reads an -ing word the verb list does not know that leads a clause as under way when the rewrite writes its -ed form ("Wiring 3 soil sensors" -> "wired"), reads set, put, cut and read in a rewrite as past when the original has them only in their -ing form ("Setting up a server" -> "set up"), and counts the Chinese status words of each kind, so a reorder may not drop 将于 beside 目前正在. An -ing word the list does not know that names a role ("Visiting student", "Fundraising chair") leads no work under way.
- `source_bullets` holds at most 6,000 characters in all, the size of 12 bullets of 500. The contract and the claim locks run on a worker with a 10 s deadline, past which the originals are kept unchecked, and the claim locks do not read main's attribution parser. `tests/test_rewrite_cpu_bounds.py` holds each unit of 14 adversarial shapes at 6,000 characters, in Tailor and full-target form, alone and with a support line, to 0.25 s of process time for the contract and the locks together, and requests at the cap through `/api/tailor`, `/api/tailor/bullet` and full target to a 0.25 s gap between `/api/tailor/status` answers. No pattern can read a run in two ways, a run of spaces or of 为 starts one scan, and a line's personal markers and settings are read once (tested to 60,000 characters).
- The selection plan's compress rewrites have no review behind them, so their gate keeps the source-checks-v3 reading of actions: team credit never hides a new action there. That gate is main's and reads none of the status rules above: it passes "Planning to recruit 30 participants" -> "Recruited 30 sleep-study participants" as main does.
- The student's profile, confirmed skills and interests stay in the prompt as direction, never evidence. After "Use kept as new originals", `source_bullets` carries each line's evidence so reviewed wording never becomes evidence; the saved draft keeps those sources across a reload.

The sections below describe the w13.5 evidence rules; where they differ from the list above (rejection behaviour, `source_evidence`), the list above applies.

## What changed

The old vocabulary check pooled a student's profile and all submitted bullets. It could accept project A's technology or number in project B, and accept “I led” when the original said “I did not lead.” Controlled provider-free HTTP tests reproduced both errors in the real routes.

Each rewrite now uses the corresponding original bullet as its evidence:

- `/tailor`: the source selected by the existing `source_index` association.
- `/tailor/renovate`: that foregrounded bullet's `base_text`; another bullet or a profile skill is not evidence of work on this project.
- `/tailor/bullet`: `base_text` when provided. The editable `current_text` is sent separately to guide phrasing, not to establish additional facts. Older callers that omit the base provide their current bullet as the only source; this is caller-supplied evidence, not independent verification.

`source_evidence` is checked against the same local original. An unrelated or fabricated quote is removed instead of displayed as proof. The known concrete-term/number validator and the same conservative EN/ZH claim locks used by full-target AI apply to these three routes. Missing detail is not filled from the student's skill list, another project or the opportunity's vocabulary.

On rejection, `/tailor` keeps its existing original fallback/partial-suggestion behavior; Renovate leaves the original bullet and its rollback floor intact; single-bullet optimization returns the user's `current_text` unchanged with `changed=false` and a warning. A rejected suggestion does not replace manual edits with the base text. Saving and confirmation flows are unchanged.

## Target relevance and missing information

Single-bullet optimization now receives the detached public target's professor name, organization and description excerpt. The description uses the existing 1,200-character budget and whitespace sanitization; When the clean field is absent, the already-public `description_raw` is allowed as a fallback; no unprojected collector record or contact-reveal payload is read here. The prompt separates target relevance from student evidence and treats these fields as data, not instructions. Existing output/token/time/provider-call budgets are unchanged.

The model should emphasize a supported action, method or result relevant to the public research description. A general skill does not establish its use on this project. When a source lacks personal role, method or a supported outcome, preserve the stated contribution; students can supply and confirm facts through the existing full-résumé supplement flow. This change does not add an automatic question generator or a professor-paper retrieval system.

## Evidence and limits

`tests/test_resume_writing_quality.py` supplies controlled model outputs through the real HTTP handlers. It checks cross-project technology/number leakage; profile-only skill transfer; English and Chinese negation/team/publication upgrades; truthful reordering; local quotations; retained manual wording; and bounded public research context. Existing parser/index/localization positive fixtures now contain the actual actions their generated examples claim. Their positive assertions remain; the old vague source-to-stronger-action pairs are retained as negative cases.

These are bounded regression checks, not semantic entailment. Exact sensitive-clause preservation can reject a valid paraphrase or translation; unlisted action synonyms, generic claims and a mismatched relationship between already-present numbers can still escape token checks. Keeping the original available is safer than silently asserting a stronger contribution. The full-target pipeline already has independent confirmed-entry evidence and uses the same claim locks. The shared helper now separates explicit English/Chinese contrast clauses, so a negative first clause cannot exempt a new affirmative responsibility/publication claim after “but” or “但”. This fix also applies to full-target suggestions and is covered by their existing regressions plus the new paired receipt cases.

No real model output or human résumé review was evaluated here. Naturalness, useful professor/lab alignment, bilingual rewrite quality and follow-up question quality still require fixed real samples with human judgments. Passing these tests does not complete M36/M38–M40 or prove that generated résumés are ready to send.
