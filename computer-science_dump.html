From AI to Robotics: Computing drives the world to come

Understand the nature of computation and explore the great potential of computers. Our faculty can teach you to precisely define and solve problems, not only in computer science but across all the arts and sciences. We’ll help you understand the core ideas of, utilize the tools from and contribute to future advances in computer science.

GPMoo! Williams’ Computing Co-op

Faculty and students are using the college’s new micro AI data center to answer questions in fields as diverse as economics, geosciences and psychology. The GPU cluster uses 30 percent less electricity than previous servers, helping Williams reduce its greenhouse gas emissions.
